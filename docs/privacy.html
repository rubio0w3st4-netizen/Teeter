Privacy Policy for Teeter

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Teeter (package com.varnok.eslin.teeter, the “Application”) handles information when You use it, and what rights You have. The Application is a single-player offline game. It does not require an account, and it does not send Your information anywhere.

Interpretation and definitions

Words with capitalised initials have the meaning set out below. The definitions apply whether they appear in singular or plural.

  • Application means Teeter, the software provided by the Developer.
  • Developer (“We”, “Us”, “Our”) means the publisher of the Application.
  • Device means any device that can run the Application, such as a phone or a tablet.
  • Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable person.
  • Service means the Application.
  • You means the individual using the Application.

Information We collect

We collect no Personal Data. The Application has no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting service. It does not read Your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, calendar, call log or installed-app list, and it does not ask for those permissions.

The Application does not transmit any data off the Device. It works with the network switched off, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Information stored on Your Device

So that progress survives between sessions, the Application saves a small amount of data in its own private storage area on the Device:

  • your game progress — per-level star ratings and blocks pulled, lifetime run, block-pull and collapse counters, your tallest tower and best endless score, unlocked awards, which in-game guides you have seen, the backdrop you have selected, whether the tutorial has been seen, and your sound and vibration preferences.

This data never leaves the Device. It is not readable by other applications. Clearing the Application’s storage, or uninstalling it, deletes all of it permanently.

Permissions

The Application requests the following Android permissions: VIBRATE, for haptic feedback when blocks slide, settle and fall; and INTERNET, which is declared only by the 3D rendering library the Application is built on (that library can load models over the network) — every model, sound and typeface in Teeter ships inside the app package, so the Application itself never opens a network connection. No other permission is requested.

Third parties

The Application contains no third-party analytics, advertising or attribution components, and We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone — because none is collected.

Children’s privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. As no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for Us to store, disclose or delete on request.

Security

Game data lives in the Device’s private application storage and is protected by the operating system’s own sandboxing. Keeping the Device updated and locked is the most effective protection for it.

Links to other sites

The Application does not contain links that open third-party websites.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. You are advised to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If You have any question about this Privacy Policy, You can reach Us by opening an issue at https://github.com/rubio0w3st4-netizen/Teeter/issues.
